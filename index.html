Welcome to G-HyQ

The Gupta Laboratory for Hybrid Quantum Systems and Devices (G-HyQ) is a research group in the Department of Physics at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. We investigate fundamental physical phenomena involving spin, orbital angular momentum, magnetism and ultrafast dynamics, with the aim of developing concepts for next-generation devices and technologies.

Our Research

Our research combines materials science, magnetism, nanofabrication, electrical measurements and ultrafast spectroscopy to investigate emerging phenomena in magnetic and quantum materials.

Spintronics & Spin-Orbitronics

We investigate generation, transport and manipulation of spin angular momentum in magnetic heterostructures, with emphasis on spin-orbit torques and energy-efficient magnetic devices.

Orbitronics

We explore orbital angular momentum transport and orbital Hall phenomena as emerging pathways for controlling magnetization and developing next-generation electronic devices.

Terahertz Spintronics

We study ultrafast spin and orbital transport and their conversion into terahertz radiation in magnetic thin films and heterostructures.

Magnetic Materials

We investigate magnetic thin films, interfaces, heterostructures and emerging materials with properties relevant to spintronic and quantum devices.

Hybrid Quantum Systems

We are interested in hybrid material platforms combining different quantum degrees of freedom and functionalities for fundamental studies and future device concepts.

Quantum & Energy-Efficient Devices

We seek to translate fundamental understanding of spin and orbital phenomena into concepts for memories, oscillators, sensors and other energy-efficient technologies.

Facilities & Experimental Capabilities

G-HyQ is developing experimental capabilities for magnetic materials, spin and orbital transport, nanostructures and ultrafast phenomena.

Thin-Film Growth

Magnetron sputtering and related thin-film deposition techniques for magnetic heterostructures and devices.

Electrical & Spin Transport

Hall measurements, spin transport, spin-orbit torque characterization and related electrical techniques.

Terahertz Spectroscopy

Ultrafast and terahertz techniques for studying spin and orbital transport and spintronic terahertz emitters.

Magnetization Dynamics

Ferromagnetic resonance and related techniques for investigating magnetization dynamics, damping and spin-torque phenomena.

Magneto-Optical Techniques

Magneto-optical Kerr effect and related optical characterization of magnetic materials.

Structural Characterization

X-ray diffraction, X-ray reflectivity and complementary techniques for thin-film structural analysis.
